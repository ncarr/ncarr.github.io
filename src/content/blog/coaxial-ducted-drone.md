- Wrote ESP32 firmware for an automated test stand to measure thrust and power consumption of coaxial propellers with varying propeller tip to duct clearances
- Wrote Pandas data aggregation code to compare test stand data in Jupyter notebooks
- Selected an autopilot, rangefinder, optical flow sensor, and propellers for the drone
- Configured ArduPilot firmware for sensors, coaxial motors, and thrust vectoring vanes
- Demonstrated takeoff, positional stabilization, and beverage can delivery
